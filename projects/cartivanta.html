01 / Overview

EVERYDAY SHOPKEEPING MEETS ENTERPRISE DEMAND INTELLIGENCE.

Designed so a store owner can operate billing and Khata without training, while exposing 6 dedicated BI analytics pages with plain-language metrics strictly in Indian Rupees (₹).

01

Barcode POS & GST Billing

Rapid checkout screen with barcode lookup, GST computation, weighted-average COGS tracking, and atomic stock deduction.

02

Khata (Udhaar) & RBAC

Tracks customer credit limits, repayments, and supplier payables with strict Owner vs Cashier role-based access control.

03

1.029M Transaction Analytics

Benchmarked over 1.029M historical retail rows delivering net sales, gross margin, and velocity insights.

04

12-Week Demand Forecasting

LightGBM and Holt-Winters time-series forecasts evaluated with WAPE & MAE accuracy metrics.

05

K-Means RFM Segmentation

Clusters customers into actionable loyalty segments paired with a Festive Discount & Margin Uplift Simulator.

06

5D Data Quality Action Center

Scores catalog/transaction completeness and anomalies at 80.9/100 with concrete inventory reorder actions.
