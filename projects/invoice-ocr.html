01 / Overview

BRIDGING UNSTRUCTURED INVOICES AND AUDITED LEDGERS.

Manual invoice entry suffers from transcription errors and duplicate disbursements. Invoice Intelligence enforces mathematical verification and human-in-the-loop confidence inspection.

01

OpenCV Image Pipeline

Performs MinAreaRect affine deskewing, bilateral edge-preserving denoising, 1.5x bicubic upscaling, and Otsu binarization.

02

Tesseract 5.4 BBox Overlay

Extracts spatially anchored tokens under PSM 4 and highlights fields Green (>=80%), Amber (50–79%), or Red (<50%).

03

Reverse-Token Table Parser

Scans invoice line items right-to-left to robustly separate quantities, unit rates, taxes, and amounts even in wrapped descriptions.

04

Math Reconciliation Gate

Validates sum(line_items) + tax against extracted invoice grand totals, routing discrepancies directly into My Work Queue.

05

Currency-Segregated AP Desk

Separates INR, USD, EUR, and GBP analytics totals accurately without mixing FX denominations.

06

Formula-Safe Excel Export

Neutralizes spreadsheet CSV/Excel formula injection payloads while emitting audit logs and normalized vendor summaries.
