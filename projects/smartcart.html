01 / Overview

OBJECTIVE BUYING INTELLIGENCE FOR INDIAN RETAILERS.

Retailers inflate MRPs to advertise fake discounts. SmartCart compares historical lows, delivery-inclusive checkout prices, and statistical forecasts across 5 Indian stores.

01

5-Retailer Comparison Matrix

Tracks Amazon India, Flipkart, Croma, Reliance Digital, and Tata CLiQ comparing base price, shipping fees, and stock status.

02

Explainable 0–100 Deal Scorer

Scores deals using Avg Proximity (35 pts), All-Time Low (25 pts), Store Spread (20 pts), MRP Depth (10 pts), and Freshness (10 pts).

03

OLS Price Forecast + 95% CI

Models 7-, 14-, and 30-day forward trajectories using linear regression with honest 95% confidence intervals.

04

APScheduler & Alert Rules

Runs background 30-minute checks evaluating TARGET_MET, PRICE_DROP, PERCENTAGE_DROP, and BACK_IN_STOCK triggers.

05

Strict Data Provenance

Explicitly stamps every quote LIVE, CACHED, HISTORICAL, SAMPLE, or UNAVAILABLE — never masquerading mocks as live scrapes.

06

Consumables & Budgets

Tracks monthly category spend caps plus restock countdown intervals alongside a PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 JWT auth layer.
