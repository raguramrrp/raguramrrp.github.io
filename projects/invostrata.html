01 / Overview

AUTOMATING FINANCIAL CONSISTENCY IN ACCOUNTS PAYABLE.

Accounts payable teams spend countless hours cross-referencing invoice line items against purchase orders and goods received notes. INVOSTRATA catches rounding errors, tax mismatches, and overbilling before payment.

01

Invoice Register Ledger

Centralized AP ledger indexing all pending and processed invoices with automated risk flags and verification badges.

02

Audit Record Inspector

Interactive line-by-line inspector displaying extracted quantities, unit prices, tax percentages, and discrepancy markers.

03

Validation Centre

Configurable audit rule suite testing subtotal sums, tax rate validity, currency consistency, and duplicate invoice numbers.

04

Exceptions Management

Dedicated dispute triage desk allowing finance managers to reject, adjust, or approve flagged invoices with audit notes.

05

3-Way Matching Engine

Automatically pairs supplier invoices with active Purchase Orders (PO) and Goods Receipt Notes (GRN) to verify delivered quantities.

06

Vendor Compliance Matrix

Tracks vendor error rates, recurring discrepancy patterns, and average invoice turnaround times.
