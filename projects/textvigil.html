01 / Overview

ACTIONABLE THREAT VERIFICATION BEFORE USERS TAP OR PAY.

Generic spam filters output a bare spam label without explaining why a message is risky or which phrase is deceptive. TextVigil exposes exact token weights, de-obfuscates raw links, decodes TRAI headers, and protects against outgoing UPI fraud.

01

iPhone 18 Pro Mobile Preview

Figma-style iPhone 18 Pro titanium hardware frame (390×844) with seamless desktop full-screen toggle and zero-clutter mobile responsiveness.

02

Three-Mode Message Scanner

Supports clipboard text paste (2,000 char max), local OpenCV + Tesseract v5 screenshot OCR with editable confirmation, and 1,000-row batch CSV scanning.

03

8-Category Threat Classification

Logistic Regression + Naive Bayes over scam-aware TF-IDF unigram/bigrams categorizing Financial Scam, Phishing, Fake Reward, Impersonation, and Credential Theft.

04

UPI / QR Payment Forensics

Local OpenCV QRCodeDetector + deep-link parser decodes payee VPA and amount, warning users that QR scans initiate outgoing debits, never deposits.

05

TRAI DLT Sender & Unicode Audit

Decodes Indian DLT telemarketer headers, flags 10-digit mobile impersonations, and detects zero-width evasion characters and Cyrillic/Greek homoglyph domain spoofs.

06

Sanchar Saathi & Golden-Hour Triage

One-tap clipboard copy formatted for Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu) and cybercrime.gov.in plus 1930 recovery checklists and defanged 'Warn Family' summaries.
