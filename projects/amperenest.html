01 / Overview

ELIMINATING POST-FACTO ELECTRICITY BILL SHOCK.

Non-linear telescopic slabs make mid-cycle bill estimation difficult for households. AmpereNest tracks daily dial or kWh readings across sub-meters and exposes exact slab progression before cycle close.

01

TANGEDCO LT-1A Engine

Computes exact non-linear residential telescopic slabs (0–100 free, 101–200, 201–400, 401–500, >500 tiers) transparently.

02

Multi-Household & Sub-Meters

Switch workspaces across properties (e.g., Main 3-Phase Domestic vs Borewell Motor sub-meter) with Owner/Viewer RBAC.

03

Dial Rollover Validation

Supports both cumulative meter dial diffs and direct kWh logs while catching duplicate dates and dial inversions.

04

Cycle-End Bill Forecasting

Projects cycle-end units and INR bills using moving averages paired with a >= 2 readings sparse-data safeguard.

05

Appliance What-If Planner

Calculates kWh drain by wattage and daily run hours so households can simulate AC/heater schedule cuts.

06

Budget & Surge Alerts

Triggers rule-based warnings when daily usage spikes unexpectedly or monthly spending crosses 80% of budget.
