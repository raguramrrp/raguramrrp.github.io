01 / Overview

FROM COARSE SEMESTER GRADES TO QUESTION-LEVEL DIAGNOSTICS.

Overall GPAs hide whether marks are lost to conceptual gaps, formula mix-ups, or exam time pressure. GradeLens classifies every topic transparently and measures post-revision recovery.

01

Question-Level Mistake Taxonomy

Logs difficulty, question type (MCQ/Numerical/Code), obtained marks, and error root causes (Conceptual, Formula, Calculation, Careless, Timing).

02

7-State Weakness Engine

Classifies topics deterministically into Repeated Weakness (<60% across >=2 exams), Weak, Monitoring, Strong (>=75%), Improving, Declining, or Insufficient Data.

03

Metacognitive Blind-Spot Matrix

Pairs student confidence (Low/Medium/High) with answer accuracy to pinpoint dangerous high-confidence errors.

04

Revision Gain Tracker

Directly compares topic accuracy before vs after logged revision sessions to prove study ROI.

05

8 SQL Views + Power BI DAX

Includes powerbi/export_powerbi.py feeding a certified 5-page Microsoft Power BI cohort and faculty executive report.

06

Student & Faculty Portals

Role-based Streamlit UI with CSV/Excel bulk import validators and explainable natural-language reasoning callouts.
