01 / Overview

DATA-DRIVEN COMPENSATION BENCHMARKING FOR TECH TALENT.

Job offers in technology are notoriously opaque, mixing base pay, joining bonuses, and paper stock options. SALVERA breaks down compensation structures with mathematical precision to reveal true take-home value.

01

Industry CTC Overview

High-level market summary tracking median developer salaries, remote work premiums, and hiring velocity across Indian tech hubs.

02

Salary Explorer Workstation

Interactive P10, P50, and P90 percentile curves plotted across experience brackets for SWE, AI/ML, and Data roles.

03

Offer Negotiation Studio

Side-by-side compensation comparison model calculating annualized in-hand pay after deducting New/Old Tax regime slabs.

04

Equity & ESOP Simulator

Vesting schedule visualizer modeling 4-year cliffs, strike price exercise costs, and potential liquidity upside.

05

Cost-of-Living Parity

Calculates purchasing power parity (PPP) between Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, and tier-2 tech clusters.

06

Job Market Intelligence

Live job listing explorer with verified salary ranges, equity flags, and transparent benefits audit.
