01 / Overview

PRIVACY-FIRST SPEND INTELLIGENCE WITH ZERO BANK CREDENTIALS.

Cryptic card statement lines hide stealth subscriptions and spending outliers. ExpenseIQ normalizes CSV statements locally on SQLite without touching cloud APIs.

01

Heuristic CSV Ingestion

Auto-detects date, description, amount, and category columns from heterogeneous bank & card exports (140+ sample rows included).

02

3-Layer Hybrid Classifier

Combines Joblib-serialized TfidfVectorizer + LogisticRegression (P >= 0.35 gate), 100+ merchant regex stems across 12 categories, and safe fallback.

03

Dual-Layer Anomalies

Flags extreme single spends via per-category Z-Score/IQR and catches multivariate timing/amount patterns via Isolation Forest.

04

Dynamic Budget Variance

Monitors monthly outlays against caps with clear Within Budget (<85%), Near Limit (85–100%), and Over Budget (>100%) badges.

05

Executive Spend Plots

Interactive Plotly daily burn rate charts, 7-day rolling averages, monthly trend bars, and category donuts.

06

100% Local Persistence

Zero-config SQLite3 storage paired with filtered CSV and JSON export deliverables.
