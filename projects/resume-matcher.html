01 / Overview

TRANSPARENT ATS SCORING BEFORE YOU HIT SUBMIT.

Applicants are frequently screened out by keyword mismatch or weak phrasing. This analyzer breaks compatibility down into semantic similarity, prioritized missing skills, and structural compliance.

01

PDF / DOCX / TXT Parser

Extracts text cleanly across formats using pypdf, python-docx, and multi-encoding UTF-8/Latin-1/CP1252 fallbacks.

02

1,200+ Skills Taxonomy

Maps competencies across 8 domains with alias resolution (e.g., k8s -> Kubernetes, react.js -> React).

03

TF-IDF Cosine Scoring

Computes sublinear term-frequency unigram + bigram vectorization for nuanced semantic similarity against target JDs.

04

Prioritized Gap Matrix

Segments missing keywords into High Priority (core repeated JD requirements), Medium Priority, and Nice to Have.

05

Power Verb & Metric Audit

Evaluates 100+ strong action verbs vs 14 passive weak phrases and scans for quantified %, currency, and scale indicators.

06

HTML & JSON Scorecards

Renders Plotly radial gauges and domain radar charts alongside standalone downloadable HTML and JSON reports.
